Fig. 02About

Two questions have held my attention since first year. How the body encodes information, and how software earns the right to be trusted with it.

Most of what I build sits on that seam. Signal processing and deep learning on one side, and the unglamorous discipline of audit logs, access control and compliance on the other. A model that is right most of the time is a paper. A system a clinician can lean on at 3 a.m. is something else.

The MedTech minor is where the two meet. It put me through radiology and imaging, healthcare informatics, clinical decision algorithms and biostatistics, and it sent me into the Emergency and Casualty department at PES Hospital. Watching triage happen under real load changed how I write software for clinicians. A form field is a decision someone has to make while a room is filling up.
